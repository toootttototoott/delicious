Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 July 2026

This policy describes how Delicious Booking and the venue operating this booking page handle personal information collected through the booking widget, booking page view, administrative portal, and related confirmation or enquiry emails.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to personal information collected from guests making a booking or enquiry, venue staff using the management portal, and business representatives administering the service.

2. Who is collecting the information

The booking service is provided through Delicious Booking for the venue or hospitality business that operates the booking page you are using. The venue remains responsible for its customer relationship and booking operations. Delicious Booking operates the software platform and supporting infrastructure used to process bookings, enquiries, availability, user access, and service emails.

3. What personal information we collect

  • Guest details such as first name, last name, email address, phone number, party size, requested date and time, and booking notes.
  • Enquiry details for larger or custom booking requests.
  • Administrative user details such as name, email address, role, and assigned venue.
  • Technical and security information such as session information, IP-derived request controls, password reset status, and system logs needed to operate and secure the service.

4. How we collect personal information

  • Directly from you when you submit a booking or enquiry form.
  • Directly from venue staff and administrators when they create or manage bookings, venues, or user accounts.
  • Indirectly through normal operation of the website and service, such as session cookies, server request metadata, and email delivery systems.

5. Why we collect personal information

  • To receive, confirm, update, manage, or cancel bookings and booking enquiries.
  • To contact guests about their reservation or enquiry.
  • To let venue staff manage availability, opening hours, and booking operations.
  • To authenticate administrative users and protect the service from misuse, fraud, and unauthorised access.
  • To send service emails such as booking confirmations and password reset links.
  • To maintain backups, security controls, and business continuity processes.

6. What happens if you do not provide the information

If required booking or enquiry information is not provided, the system may be unable to create, hold, update, or confirm the reservation, or the venue may be unable to contact you about it.

7. Who we disclose personal information to

  • The venue and its authorised staff who operate the booking calendar you are using.
  • Service providers used to host, secure, support, and back up the platform.
  • Email delivery providers used to send booking confirmations, enquiries, and password reset emails.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law enforcement, or courts where required or authorised by law.

8. Overseas disclosure

The production booking database is hosted in Sydney, Australia. Some supporting service providers used by the platform, including hosting, email, and software tooling providers, may process or store limited personal information outside Australia. Where personal information is disclosed to overseas service providers, reasonable contractual and operational steps should be taken to protect that information in line with applicable Australian privacy obligations.

9. Storage, security, and backups

Guest booking fields stored by the application are encrypted before being written to the database. Data is transmitted to the database over encrypted connections. Administrative access uses authenticated server-side sessions. The production database uses automated daily backups, and restore testing has been performed operationally. Reasonable technical and organisational steps are used to reduce the risk of misuse, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure.

10. Retention

Personal information is retained for as long as it is reasonably required for booking operations, customer service, dispute handling, legal compliance, security, backup cycles, and related business records. Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or de-identified in line with the Data Retention and Deletion Policy for this service.

11. Access and correction

You may request access to personal information held about you, or ask for corrections if you believe the information is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. Requests should be directed to the venue you booked with or to the privacy contact published for this deployment.

12. Complaints

Privacy complaints should first be sent to the privacy contact for the venue or service deployment so the issue can be investigated and responded to. If the matter is not resolved, individuals may have rights to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

13. Notifiable data breaches

If this service experiences an eligible data breach and the Privacy Act 1988 applies, affected individuals and the OAIC must be notified where the breach is likely to result in serious harm. The operational response process is documented in the internal Data Breach Response Plan.

14. Marketing

This booking application is configured for operational emails such as confirmations, enquiries, and password resets. If marketing emails are introduced later, consent, sender identification, contact details, and unsubscribe requirements should be met under Australian spam laws before those messages are sent.
