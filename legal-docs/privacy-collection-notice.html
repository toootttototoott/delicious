Privacy Collection Notice

This notice applies when you submit a booking or enquiry through this booking page.

Who is collecting your information

Your information is collected by the venue operating this booking page and processed through the Delicious Booking platform on the venue's behalf.

What information is collected

We collect your name, email address, phone number, party size, requested booking date and time, and any booking notes or enquiry details you provide.

Why we collect it

We collect this information so the venue can assess, confirm, manage, update, or contact you about your booking or enquiry, and so the service can operate securely and reliably.

If you do not provide it

If you do not provide the requested information, the venue may be unable to process or confirm your booking or enquiry.

Who we may disclose it to

  • The venue and authorised venue staff.
  • Service providers that host, secure, back up, support, or email from the platform.
  • Regulators, advisers, insurers, or authorities where required or authorised by law.

Overseas disclosure

The production booking database is hosted in Sydney, Australia. Some supporting providers used to operate the platform may process limited information outside Australia.
