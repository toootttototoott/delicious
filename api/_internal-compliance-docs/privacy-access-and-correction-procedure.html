Internal Procedure

Privacy Access and Correction Procedure

Version 1.0 | Effective date: 9 July 2026

1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the business will handle requests from individuals seeking access to personal information held about them or seeking correction of personal information.

2. Intake channels

Requests may be received by email, contact form, support channel, or directly from a venue where the request relates to a booking made through the platform.

3. Verification

Before disclosing, amending, or deleting personal information, take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requester and confirm that the person is authorised to make the request.

4. Access requests

  • Log the date received, requester identity, and scope of the request.
  • Search the relevant booking, enquiry, user, and related system records.
  • Assess whether any information should be withheld under an applicable exception or because disclosure would unreasonably affect another person's privacy.
  • Respond in a timely manner and, where practicable, within 30 calendar days.
  • Provide the information in a practical format, such as a secure email summary or exported record.

5. Correction requests

  • Confirm what information is said to be inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading.
  • Check the underlying record and supporting evidence.
  • If correction is justified, update the record promptly and note the change.
  • If the request is refused, provide written reasons and explain complaint options.

6. Escalation

Escalate unusual, high-risk, or disputed requests to the responsible privacy or business lead, especially where the request overlaps with a complaint, dispute, or legal issue.

7. Documentation

Keep a record of the request, verification steps, searches performed, decision made, response date, and any corrections or disclosures completed.
